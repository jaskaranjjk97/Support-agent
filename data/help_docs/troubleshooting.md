# Troubleshooting

## Can't log in
- Check email spelling and caps lock.
- Use "Forgot password" to reset.
- If SSO is enforced, contact your workspace admin.

## Tasks not syncing
- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Clear browser cache.

## Slow performance
- Large boards (>5000 tasks) may lag. Use filters.
- Contact support if issues persist for more than 24 hours.

## Known issues
- Safari 16 has a rendering bug with the Gantt view. Use Chrome or Firefox.
