# Billing & Plans

## Available plans
- Starter: INR 799/month, up to 5 seats
- Pro: INR 1499/month or INR 14999/year, up to 30 seats
- Enterprise: custom pricing, unlimited seats

## Updating payment method
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Method. We accept cards and UPI.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry for 3 days. After that, the account moves to
"past_due" and features become read-only until payment succeeds.

## Refunds
Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for annual plans. Contact
support with your order ID.
