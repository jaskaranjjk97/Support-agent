# Integrations

TaskFlow integrates with:
- Slack: post task updates to channels
- GitHub: link commits and PRs to tasks
- Google Calendar: sync sprint deadlines
- Zapier: connect 5000+ apps

## Setting up Slack
1. Go to Settings > Integrations > Slack.
2. Click "Connect" and authorize the TaskFlow app.
3. Choose a default channel for notifications.

## Webhooks
Enterprise customers can configure outbound webhooks at Settings > Developer.
